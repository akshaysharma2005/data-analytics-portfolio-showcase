Machine Learning Architecture & Data Pipeline

1. Automated Data Wrangling

Web-scraped listings underwent deduplication, regular expression normalization of text descriptions, and KNN missing value imputation for ambiguous bathrooms and floor levels.

2. Feature Engineering

Synthesized 8+ derived indicators: area per room ratio, high floor binary indicator, log-transformed area to eliminate extreme tail skew, and district frequency encodings.

3. Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP)

Feed-forward neural architecture with ReLU activations, Adam optimizer, early stopping regularization, and MinMax scaling on continuous variables.

4. VotingRegressor Ensemble

Averaging the predictions of Gradient Boosting Trees with the MLP Neural Network lowered variance and achieved the optimal test RMSE of 116,478 PLN (MSLE: 0.03).

Ensemble Aggregation Formula:
y_ensemble = 0.5 * y_GBR(x) + 0.5 * y_MLP(x)
